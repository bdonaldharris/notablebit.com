What I noticed in the current build

  1. 01 · globals.css :root, body, .site-shellToo many colors and layers at onceThere are 9 color tokens (blue, violet, amber, burgundy, cream, green…), 4 radial glows, a grid overlay and a woven texture behind every page. Your preferences ask for restraint and no visual interruption. The design-direction doc lists these as accents to use sparingly, but right now they all show up together.
  2. 02 · logo-white-text.png vs --blueThe site doesn't use the logo's colorsThe logo mark is red #F9313C and blue #014DF9. The site uses #0066FF, and the red appears nowhere. The physical studio (white walls, red and blue on screen) is lighter and warmer than the site.
  3. 03 · .home-hero-copy .lede { margin-top: 204px }Large empty gap in the heroThere's 204px of space between the headline and the line that explains it, so the reader loses the thread. Below the hero, the mission card, ecosystem core and founder band each have their own gradient panel at about the same visual weight, so nothing stands out as most important.
  4. 04 · page.tsx missionOutputs · layout.tsx ecosystemLogosEcosystem order and names are inconsistentThe diagram reads BitVoices → HindSite → Advisory → Studio, and the footer starts with B Donald Harris. Your established order is NotableBIT | BIT Voices Podcast | BitVoices Network | HindSite. Also, "Start with BitVoices" links to /media, which mixes up the podcast and the network.
  5. 05 · .home-type-* (5 lab classes)The typeface decision is still openFive type experiments are still in the CSS. The body font, Aptos, is a Microsoft font, so most Mac and phone visitors see a fallback font instead. Choosing one hosted font pair would make the site look the same for everyone.
  6. 06 · 08-notablebit.mdThe homepage doesn't say what the studio doesYour own description of the work — idea → … → verified deployment, and helping people become better builders — doesn't appear on the homepage. 1b adds it as one band, written in your own words. It's new content, so tell me if you'd rather leave it out.

1b Liner Notes: light, editorial, using the logo's two colors

The bigger change. A warm paper background and near-black text, plus exactly two accents: the logo's red and blue. Red marks voice and media (podcast, Listen); blue marks action and building (buttons, links, deployment). The palette matches the room you actually record in. Type is Newsreader for headlines, IBM Plex Sans for body text and Plex Mono for labels. The layout is modeled on album liner notes: a tracklist for the ecosystem, numbered steps for how you build, and captions under photos. Tradeoff: this moves away from the "dark navy foundation" in notablebit-design-direction.md §5, which you'd need to update.

1c Signal: keeps the dark theme, with far fewer colors and effects

The smaller change, and it stays within the current design-direction doc. Keeps the dark base, electric blue and institutional gold. Cuts violet, burgundy, green, every radial glow, the grid and the woven texture. The background is a flat near-black instead of navy gradients. The blue is changed to the logo's blue. Gold is used only for labels and the final "Deployment" step, never for buttons. Type is Source Serif for headlines and Schibsted Grotesk for body text. The hero headline and its explanation sit together, and the ecosystem appears in your established order right in the hero.
